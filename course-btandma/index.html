Business Taxes and Mergers & Acquisitions

Dr. Matthijs Breugem — Nyenrode Business University · m.breugem@nyenrode.nl

This course introduces corporate valuation, business taxation, and mergers & acquisitions, combining theory with practical application. Students learn to analyze financial statements, estimate cost of capital, and evaluate projects using NPV, IRR, and free cash flow. The course covers local and international business taxation, exploring both rules and economic implications. Through the multi-stage PowerBev simulation, we motivate the need for business M&A in a case where students practice negotiation, collaboration, and synergy assessment, linking valuation and strategic decision-making. The M&A module examines motivations, deal logistics, and takeover strategies, reinforced with practical cases.

Course modules

  • 1. Corporate Valuation — financial statements, cost of capital, and business valuation with the MENSAM and PINGGUO cases.
  • 2. Business Taxes — business taxation in the Netherlands (ZZP, BV, NV, the box system) and global taxation, avoidance & ethics.
  • Simulation — the multi-round Power Beverage business strategy simulation.
  • 3. M&A — motives, synergy valuation, and 130 years of merger waves.
  • Solutions — worked solutions to the taxation exercises, plus links to the case spreadsheets.
