Business Simulation: Power Beverage

A multi-round business strategy exercise

The Power Beverage Simulation is an interactive, multi-round business strategy exercise in which student teams manage competing firms in a dynamic market environment.

Logistics and Rules

  • The simulation will take place over multiple rounds, with each round requiring strategic decisions from your team.
  • Decision deadlines will be communicated during the plenary sessions or group meetings.
  • Deadlines are strict. Submissions received after the stated time will be invalid — late entries are not accepted.
  • Please do not communicate across groups. Sharing information between teams will negatively affect your team's performance.

Grading and Attendance

  • Participation (group + individual) in the simulation counts for 40% of your final grade.
  • Attendance at at least 3 out of 4 simulation sessions (lectures 7, 8, 9, and 10) is mandatory. Students attending fewer than three sessions must retake the case next year.
  • A group exercise related to the case — also part of the 40% grade component — will be posted after Lecture 10. Submission deadlines will be announced on Canvas.

Final Note

  • This is a game designed to challenge your analytical thinking, teamwork, and strategic insight.
  • Have fun — and good luck!
